[285] The Messenger has believed in what was revealed to him from his Lord, and [so have] the believers. All of them have believed in Allah and His angels and His books and His messengers, saying, "We make no distinction between any of His messengers." And they say, "We hear and we obey. Grant us Your forgiveness, our Lord, and to You is the final destination."

Rinse Mouth (3×)

Take water into the mouth and rinse it around 3 times, then spit it out.

5

Sniff Water into Nose (3×)

Sniff water into the nose and blow it out 3 times. Use the left hand to blow.

6

Wash Face (3×)

Wash the entire face 3 times — from hairline to chin, and from ear to ear.

7

Wash Arms to Elbows (3×)

Wash right arm first from fingertips to elbow 3 times, then left arm the same way.

8

Wipe Head (1×)

Wipe the entire head once with wet hands — from front to back and back to front. Then wipe both ears inside and outside.

9

Wash Feet to Ankles (3×)

Wash right foot 3 times including between toes and up to the ankle. Then left foot. Make sure water reaches all areas.

10

Completion Dua

أَشْهَدُ أَنْ لَّا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا اللهُ وَحْدَهُ لَا شَرِيكَ لَهُ وَأَشْهَدُ أَنَّ مُحَمَّدًا عَبْدُهُ وَرَسُولُهُ ۞ اللَّهُمَّ اجْعَلْنِي مِنَ التَّوَّابِينَ وَاجْعَلْنِي مِنَ الْمُتَطَهِّرِينَ

The 8 gates of Jannah open for the one who says this. — Muslim 1/209

How to Pray — Fard Salah Steps

1

Stand Facing Qibla — Niyyah

Stand upright facing the Qibla. Make intention for which prayer you are performing.

2

Takbeer ul-Ihram

اللهُ أَكْبَرُ

Raise both hands to ear level, say "Allahu Akbar" — the prayer has begun. Men: up to ears. Women: up to shoulders.

3

Qiyam — Standing

سُبْحَانَكَ اللَّهُمَّ وَبِحَمْدِكَ...

Recite opening dua (Thana) silently, then Ta'awwudh, then Surah Al-Fatiha. In first two rakats add another Surah.

4

Ruku — Bowing

سُبْحَانَ رَبِّيَ الْعَظِيمِ (×٣)

Say Allahu Akbar and bow. Hands on knees, back straight. Say "SubhanaRabbiyal Azeem" 3 times minimum.

5

I'tidal — Rising from Ruku

سَمِعَ اللهُ لِمَنْ حَمِدَهُ ۞ رَبَّنَا وَلَكَ الْحَمْدُ

Rise saying "Sami'allahu liman hamidah", then "Rabbana wa lakal-hamd" while standing fully upright.

6

First Sujood — Prostration

سُبْحَانَ رَبِّيَ الْأَعْلَى (×٣)

Allahu Akbar, go down to prostration on 7 parts: forehead+nose, 2 hands, 2 knees, toes. Say "SubhanaRabbiyal A'la" 3 times.

7

Jalsah — Sitting Between Sujood

رَبِّ اغْفِرْ لِي

Rise from first sujood, sit briefly saying "Rabbi-ghfirli". Then go for the second sujood.

8

Second Sujood

Perform the second sujood same as the first. This completes one rak'at.

9

Tashahhud

التَّحِيَّاتُ لِلَّهِ وَالصَّلَوَاتُ وَالطَّيِّبَاتُ، السَّلَامُ عَلَيْكَ أَيُّهَا النَّبِيُّ وَرَحْمَةُ اللهِ وَبَرَكَاتُهُ...

After 2nd rak'at, sit for Tashahhud. Raise index finger when saying "la ilaha illAllah".

10

Salaam — Ending the Prayer

السَّلَامُ عَلَيْكُمْ وَرَحْمَةُ اللهِ

Turn head to the right saying Assalamu Alaykum Wa Rahmatullah, then to the left. The prayer is complete.

Ramadan & Fasting

Month of Quran & Mercy

Ramadan is the month in which the Quran was revealed — a month whose beginning is mercy, whose middle is forgiveness, and whose end is freedom from the Fire.

Niyyat for Fasting — Intention made at night or before Fajr
"I intend to fast tomorrow in the month of Ramadan, as an obligation for You, O Allah — so accept it from me." The intention may be made once for the whole month in the heart; the heart's resolve is sufficient.

Dua at Iftar — Breaking the Fast

The Dua of Laylatul Qadr — The Night of Decree
Aisha RA asked: "O Messenger of Allah, if I know which night is Laylatul Qadr, what should I say?" He ﷺ said: "Say: O Allah, You are Pardoning and You love pardon, so pardon me." Seek it in the odd nights of the last ten.

Virtues of Ramadan — Quick Guide
The Quran was sent down in this month (2:185). Every night of Ramadan, Allah frees a million people from the Fire. Fasting is for Allah and He rewards it directly — "Every deed of the son of Adam is for him, except fasting; it is for Me and I will reward it." Charity in Ramadan is multiplied 70 times.
